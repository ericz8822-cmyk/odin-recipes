Reicpe of Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made by layering lasagna noodles with a rich meat and tomato sauce, creamy ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella and Parmesan. The layers are baked together until the cheese is golden and bubbly, then allowed to rest briefly before serving. It is a hearty, flavorful meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground Beef
  • Italian sausage
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Italian seasoning
  • Dried oregano
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Olive oil
  • Fresh basil or parsley (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain them.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan and sauté the onion and garlic until softened.
  4. Add the ground beef and Italian sausage, and cook until browned.
  5. Stir in the tomato sauce, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for about 15–20 minutes.
  6. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with the egg and a little Parmesan cheese.
  7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  8. Add a layer of lasagna noodles.
  9. Spread some of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
  10. Sprinkle mozzarella and Parmesan cheese over the ricotta.
  11. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used, finishing with mozzarella and Parmesan on top.
  12. Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake for about 25 minutes.
  13. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden.
  14. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
