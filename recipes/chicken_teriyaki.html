Reicpe of Chicken Teriyaki

Chicken teriyaki is a popular Japanese-inspired dish featuring tender chicken coated in a sweet and savory sauce made from soy sauce, mirin, and sugar. It is commonly served with steamed white rice and garnished with green onions and sesame seeds.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs or chicken breasts
  • Soy sauce
  • Mirin
  • Sugar
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Vegetable oil
  • Green onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Cooked white rice

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Mix soy sauce, mirin, sugar, garlic, and ginger to make the teriyaki sauce.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Add the chicken and cook until browned and fully cooked.
  5. Pour the teriyaki sauce into the pan and simmer until it thickens and coats the chicken.
  6. Remove the chicken from the heat and garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.
  7. Serve the chicken over cooked white rice.
